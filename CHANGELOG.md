# Changelog

Release notes for every version are generated automatically on the
[Releases page](https://github.com/devgalib/QuickTube/releases) from the merged pull requests.

## 1.0 (first public release)

- Search, trending and music feeds; video pages with comments and related videos
- Download video (144p–4K) and audio from YouTube and 1,800+ sites (yt-dlp engine, daily updates)
- Share-to-download from any app; in-app browser that detects the video you're watching (incl. HLS)
- Download manager: pause/resume, speed limit, Wi-Fi/mobile rules, notifications
- Library, player with background audio, vault, recycle bin, deleted-download history, WhatsApp status saver
- Themes: light/dark/system, 7 accent colours, AMOLED black, Material You
